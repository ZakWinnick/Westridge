Safety

Our Safety Standard

Safety is our first filter. Every operator, aircraft and crew is reviewed before we recommend a flight, and anything short of our standard is never offered.

What We Check on Every Operator

01

Part 135 Certificate

A current FAA air carrier certificate, verified directly with the FAA.

02

Operations Specifications

Confirmation that your aircraft appears on the operator's operations specifications, and that the journey is one they are authorized to fly.

03

ARGUS or Wyvern Data

A current independent safety rating, with a trip-specific report on the operator, aircraft and crew.

04

FAA Enforcement History

A review of accident, incident and enforcement records for both operator and aircraft.

05

Crew Experience

Two pilots, each rated and current in the aircraft type, with experience that meets our minimums. [SET MINIMUM HOURS]

06

Insurance

A current certificate of insurance, with liability coverage that meets our minimum. [SET MINIMUM LIMIT]

What We Decline

Unlicensed Charter
Any flight offered as charter without a Part 135 certificate behind it.
Unlisted Aircraft
Aircraft that do not appear on the operator's operations specifications.
Incomplete Records
Operators unwilling to share certificates, audits or proof of insurance.
Narrow Margins
Itineraries that would press crew duty limits or aircraft performance for the runway and conditions.
Unanswered Questions
Operators with unresolved enforcement actions, or a safety record we cannot fully account for.
