Common Questions

Does Westridge Own Aircraft?
No. Westridge is an air charter broker. We act as your agent, and every flight is operated by an FAA-certificated or foreign-licensed operator that holds operational control.
Do I Need a Membership?
No. Charter is booked one trip at a time, with nothing to join.
How Much Notice Do You Need?
We need at least four hours before departure. More notice means more choice of aircraft.
Where Can I Fly?
Anywhere a certificated operator can take you. We source aircraft from operators worldwide.
Can I Bring My Pet?
In most cases, yes. Tell us when you request a quote and we will choose an operator that welcomes them.
