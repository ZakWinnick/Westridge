Destinations / Tahoe

Lake Tahoe

A short flight from the Bay Area to the lake, the slopes and the quiet of the Sierra. At this altitude, the choice of airport and aircraft matters.

Flight Time From the Bay Area
About 35 to 45 Minutes
Airports
Truckee, South Lake Tahoe, Reno
Typical Aircraft
Turboprop or Light Jet

Airports

Truckee Tahoe (KTRK)
The north shore gateway, minutes from Truckee, Northstar and Palisades. At this elevation, summer heat and winter weather can limit what an aircraft may carry.
Lake Tahoe (KTVL)
The south shore gateway, closest to Heavenly and Stateline.
Reno-Tahoe International (KRNO)
About an hour by car. Longer runways and instrument approaches make Reno the dependable alternative when mountain weather turns.

Typical Aircraft

Turboprop
Seats up to eight. Ideally suited to the short flight and the mountain runways.
Light Jet
Four to six guests with weekend luggage.
Midsize Jet
Larger parties, ski bags or onward travel. In winter, most often through Reno.
